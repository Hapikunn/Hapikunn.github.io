About

I work where AI meets living systems. My research asks how genome language models can go beyond predicting sequences — toward designing them — and how to do that safely as biology becomes increasingly automated.

My long-term vision is “Text to Life”: describe a desired biological function, let AI design the life system that realizes it, and verify and improve it through experiments.

Research

Japanese genome language model — ALIS, National Institute of Genetics (2026– )

Contributing to the development of a Japanese genome language model, spanning model fine-tuning, downstream-task design, and security considerations for genome language models.

Wet/dry integrated functional DNA design — Kiga Laboratory, Waseda University (2024– )

Graduate researcher and lab leader (supervisor: Prof. Daisuke Kiga). Synthetic biology research including promoter engineering, cell-free (TX-TL) experiments, genetic-circuit construction, and bioinformatics analysis of biological sequence data. Developed a low-cost LoRA fine-tuning strategy that leverages Evo pretrained representations to build task-specific genome language models from limited labeled data.

AIP Challenge Research Grant FY2025 — “Establishment of a Wet/Dry Integrated Cycle for Developing Novel Functional DNA.” Patent application filed September 9, 2026; preprint in preparation.

Functional-constraint learning in genome language models — ExCELLS (2026– )

Seed Discovery Research Project, proposal 26EXC206 (PI: Prof. Daisuke Kiga). Studying functional-constraint learning in genome language models using artificial transcription-factor data, and investigating the physical/chemical basis of learned sequence features. Building fine-tuned genome language models and applying sparse-autoencoder analysis to compare representations of artificial versus natural sequence/function patterns.

Laboratory biosecurity — JST-RISTEX Science Impact Lab (2026)

Analyzing where biosafety and biosecurity risks emerge across stakeholders and handoffs in increasingly automated biological experimentation, including cloud-lab scenarios. Developed Lab Risk Explorer, a software prototype for mapping risk scenarios, safeguards, and changes in risk as experimental steps move from human execution to automated systems.

Cybersecurity × evolutionary biology — SecHack365, NICT (2025)

Completed the year-long Thought-Driven Course with a project applying co-evolution and phylogenetic concepts to cybersecurity — modeling interactions between red-team AI and blue-team AI to visualize how attack patterns evolve.
